The Premier Gateway for U.S.–China Enterprise

America-China.com is a division of China Business Associates, Inc. (CBA). America-China.com was formed to extend the existing success that CBA has experienced in helping U.S. companies do business in China.

CBA has been promoting American interests in the People's Republic of China for over 20 years, beginning with the first historical trade mission after President Nixon normalized relations with China. CBA has advanced management and human resources, including experienced experts from all fields. CBA has gained in-depth knowledge and expertise about how to do business in China, and how to facilitate connections between America and China.

CBA also assists in forming joint or wholly-owned ventures, managing technology transfer and licensing, establishing distribution, and serving as a buyer's representative to facilitate sourcing from China. We also conduct market research, provide human resource training, lead industry delegations, and undertake special projects.

From the Managing Director's Desk: "An Apprenticeship in China"

Raja Magasweran — Co-Founder & Managing Director

In 1972, I left home for my adventure in China. I was one of a group of nine students from Sri Lanka who, along with our classmates from North Korea, Pakistan, Albania, North Vietnam, and Africa, were on scholarship at the language institute in Beijing. None of us could have foreseen that 28 years later Greater China would be referred to as the Fourth Pole in the international business community.

In 1977, my close student friends became senior officials in the various government ministries. I was in a position to convert the years of learning to earning. American companies in China needed contacts made, problems solved, and leads followed. In 1980, CHINA BUSINESS ASSOCIATES (CBA) became the first permanent American company registered in China.

China's economic development is a continuation of the convergence between Western management systems, technical expertise, and capital, and Chinese entrepreneurial energy, markets, and talent. CBA is recognized as a 'good friend' for the long term by Chinese companies and authorities.
